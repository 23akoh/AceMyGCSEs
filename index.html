🧠 High-Yield Revision Techniques

  • The Feynman Technique: Pick a topic you struggle with, review it, then attempt to write an explanation of it as if you were teaching it to an 8-year-old. This highlights gaps in your logic.
  • The Pomodoro System: Work in intervals matching your chosen custom slot lengths, followed by a dedicated rest break to clear mental fatigue.
  • Flashcards & Leitner Box: Move cards you get wrong into boxes reviewed daily, while correctly answered cards move to boxes reviewed less frequently.
  • The Blurting Method: Set a timer for 5 minutes, read a section of your notes, hide it, and instantly write down everything you remember onto a blank canvas page.

🏃 Mental Health & Focus Architecture

  • Hydration Speedups: Drinking a full glass of water right when waking up fights dehydration fatigue instantly.
  • Active Posture Breaks: Spend your customized break windows standing, walking, or getting fresh air rather than looking down at a mobile phone screen.
  • Blue Light Interceptions: Power down study tabs and revision videos 45 minutes before bedtime to prevent melatonin suppression.
