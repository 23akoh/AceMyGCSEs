🧠 High-Yield Active Revision Techniques

  • The Blurting Method: Read an exam board spec heading for 5 minutes, close your folder, and quickly write down every detail onto a blank paper canvas in red ink. Cross-reference with your revision guide afterwards.
  • The Feynman Technique: Attempt to explain a complex topic (like Mitosis or Fractional Indices) to someone who doesn't study it. If you stumble, look back at your textbook notes immediately.
  • Leitner Flashcard Boxes: Organise flashcards into three boxes. Review Box 1 every day, Box 2 every three days, and Box 3 once a week. Cards you get wrong go back to Box 1!
  • Past Paper Dissection: Do not just read answers. Attempt the question first, mark it harshly with the exam board criteria, and create a "mistakes log book" for quick revision before exams.

🏃 Cognitive Wellness Rules

  • Hydration Recovery: Dehydration reduces overall processing precision by up to 10%. Drink water at the beginning of each slot.
  • Active Break Intervals: Get up, stretch, or step outside during your breaks. Avoid scrolling on social media, as it keeps your brain engaged instead of resting.
  • Screen Curfew Thresholds: Turn off all digital screens and bright revision monitors at least 45 minutes before bedtime to ensure high-quality sleep.
