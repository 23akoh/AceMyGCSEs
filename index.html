⚡ Proactive Revision Methods

  • The Blurting Method: Review a subtopic syllabus for 5 minutes, close the resource entirely, then quickly blurt out every fact onto blank card stock using a distinctive ink layer color to target knowledge gaps.
  • Dual Coding Strategy: Pair diagrams, timeline paths, and scientific formulas with explicit annotations to lock memory links.
  • Spacing Effect Rules: Distribute your revision blocks across weeks instead of overnight cramming windows to keep content fresh in your long-term memory.

🏃 Physical Health Rules

  • Hydration Optimization: Drink water before your morning study window to enhance processing speeds.
  • Active Break Spans: Stand up, stretch, or walk outside during breaks to clear your mind between focus slots.
  • Screen Sparing Rules: Turn off your screens at least 45 minutes before bedtime to ensure your brain gets restful sleep.
